# Conductor — Brand Spec

Conductor inherits the MehtaCognition brand. This file is the lightweight reference for anyone customizing the bundle for their own organization.

## Voice

- **Direct-first, not warm-first.** Earn trust by pushing, not by soothing.
- **Reframing is the signature move.** Replace the comfortable diagnosis with the honest one.
- **Anti-consultant consultant.** No to-do lists, templates, packaged frameworks, or 50-item plans.
- **Diagnosis over prescription.** Walk in with better questions, not answers.
- **Names what people feel but haven't articulated.** Give language to lived experience.
- **Normalizes discomfort** as evidence the process is working.
- **Lived-in-the-chair credibility.** Speak from experience, not from theory.

When customizing skills for your own organization, keep this voice. Generic AI assistant voice ("I'd be happy to help you with...", "Great question!", "Here are some thoughts...") is the opposite of what Conductor is for. Strip it out wherever you find it.

## Naming

- **The bundle:** *Conductor* (capitalized, no qualifier)
- **The publisher:** *MehtaCognition* (one word, no space)
- **The repo:** `mc-conductor`
- **Each skill:** functional name (e.g., *Morning Brief*, *Weekly 1:1*, *Coaching Diagnostic*) — not themed-cute names. Clarity at the install moment beats branding cleverness.
- **The relationship to deeper installs:** Conductor is the portable prompt bundle. It can later grow into a private MehtaCognition setup with deeper data integration, but the public bundle runs anywhere.

## Visual

- **Primary font:** Montserrat (Bold/ExtraBold for display, Regular for body, Light for secondary text and the "COGNITION" wordmark when MC branding is shown).
- **Primary color:** Teal `#4DAAAA` (60% of any visual surface). The signature.
- **Secondary color:** Blue `#4A7BB5` (25%).
- **Accents (15% combined):** Pink `#D4577A`, Purple `#7B68AE`, Green `#4CAF50`, Gold `#D4A843`. Use sparingly.
- **Backgrounds:** Dark `#1A1A2E`, Light `#F8F9FA`. Teal on dark = signature.
- **Vibe:** Professional, modern, clean. Restraint over flash.

For public customization, use this file as the canonical lightweight visual reference. Internal MehtaCognition brand assets are not required to use Conductor.

## Quality benchmark

Look at [isca.mehtacognition.com](https://isca.mehtacognition.com). That's the standard. Anything we ship under the Conductor name should hold that bar.

## When customizing for your own org

When you fork Conductor for your school or organization, you may want to:

- Change `MehtaCognition` references in each skill to your own organization's name
- Adapt the closing attribution
- Replace teal/blue with your institution's brand colors
- Adapt the *voice* notes above to match your own leadership patterns

Keep the bones intact: the cadence rhythm, the diagnostic structure, the 1:1 dashboard, the reframing posture. Those are the load-bearing elements that make the bundle work.
